About AgriPrice Malawi

National Agricultural Market Intelligence System

AgriPrice Malawi is the sovereign decision-support platform engineered to transform grain marketing, storage economics, and food security planning across all 28 districts and key urban centers of Malawi.

Specializing strictly in Malawi's core commodities—Maize (Chimanga), Rice (Mpunga), and Beans (Nyemba)—the platform fuses time-series econometric forecasting (Prophet + SARIMAX) with empirical ground data from the Ministry of Agriculture, FAMIS Malawi, and WFP VAM.

Key Objectives:
Eliminate smallholder price distress selling during post-harvest gluts.
Promote airtight hermetic PICS bag storage with quantified monthly ROI.
Optimize inter-district transport freight corridors (M1 & M5 highways) for cooperatives and traders.
